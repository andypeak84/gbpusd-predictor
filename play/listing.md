## Full description (max 4000)
GBP/USD Predictor gives you a daily, 1-to-7-day statistical forecast for the pound against the US dollar, and shows
you openly how often it has been right.

WHAT YOU GET
- 1 to 7 day forecasts with a typical-error band, and a simple "wait / convert now / looks stable" hint.
- A built-in Accuracy screen: every past forecast scored against what the rate actually did, so you can judge it
  yourself. The forecasts are right roughly half the time. Treat them as one input, not an answer.
- A rate chart with the forecast plotted on it, and an optional "I want to convert £X" amount.
- Optional news sentiment (experimental, off by default): headlines from the Bank of England, the Federal Reserve and
  other public news feeds are scored with hand-written rules and can nudge the forecast. The screen always shows the
  forecast with and without the news, so you can see exactly what it changed.
- A home-screen widget and a daily notification.
- Live-rate price alerts (optional): bring your own free Alpha Vantage key and set a level or an hourly move.

HOW IT WORKS
A gradient-boosted decision-tree model, trained on years of daily data (exchange rates, UK and US interest rates,
volatility, oil prices and more), runs entirely on your phone. Your history stays on your device.

IMPORTANT
This app is for information only and is not financial advice. Forecasts are statistical estimates and are often wrong.
Past accuracy does not predict future results. Do not make financial decisions based on this app.

Contains ads (Google AdMob). In the UK and EEA you are asked for consent first.

Data sources: European Central Bank reference rates (via Frankfurter), US Treasury, FRED (this product uses the FRED API
but is not endorsed or certified by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis), OilPriceAPI (www.oilpriceapi.com) for Brent crude, Bank of England.
