GBP/USD Predictor: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

In short: the app has no accounts and the developer collects no personal data. Your forecast history stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID; in the UK and EEA you are asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time.

Who we are

GBP/USD Predictor (package com.andypeak.gbpusdpredictor) is published by its independent developer (contact below). Contact: gdpusd26@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your device

  • A daily history of exchange-rate and market figures and the app's forecasts (so it can show its own accuracy).
  • Your settings: the news-sentiment on/off switch and weighting sliders, and price-alert thresholds.
  • If you choose to use price alerts, the free Alpha Vantage API key you type in. It is stored only on your device and sent only to Alpha Vantage to fetch the exchange rate.

This information never leaves your device unless you use the app's Export button to save a copy yourself. Uninstalling the app deletes it. The developer has no server that receives your data and no way to see it.

What the app downloads

To work, the app makes ordinary internet requests to public data sources. They see your IP address, as any website does, but the app sends them no account, name or other identifier:

  • raw.githubusercontent.com: public data files published by the developer (oil prices, US yields and VIX, UK gilt yields, news sentiment).
  • api.frankfurter.dev: European Central Bank reference exchange rates.
  • bankofengland.co.uk: UK Bank Rate.
  • alphavantage.co: only if you added your own key for price alerts.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about your device and how you interact with ads, to show ads and to measure and prevent fraud. The developer does not receive this data and cannot identify you from it.

  • UK and EEA: when you first open the app you are shown Google's consent form, and ads are requested only after you have made a choice. You can review or change it any time under Diagnostics, history & credits → Privacy & ad choices.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • More information: how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app posts the daily forecast, news-sentiment summary and any price alerts you set up. They are generated on your phone; no notification service receives your data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is intended for adults (18+).

Not financial advice

The app is for information only. Forecasts are statistical estimates and are often wrong. It does not give financial, investment or trading advice.
